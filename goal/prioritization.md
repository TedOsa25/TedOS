# Goal Prioritization

## Purpose

Determine which goals should be executed first.

---

## Priority Order

1. Critical Security
2. Production Incidents
3. Customer Blocking Issues
4. Revenue Opportunities
5. Strategic Product Goals
6. UX Improvements
7. Technical Debt
8. Documentation

---

## Decision Factors

Evaluate:

* Business Impact
* Customer Impact
* Risk
* Revenue
* Effort
* Dependencies
* Urgency

---

## Output

Each goal receives:

* Priority Score
* Execution Order
* Recommended Timeline
