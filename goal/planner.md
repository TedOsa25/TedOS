# Goal Planner

## Purpose

The Goal Planner converts a parsed goal into an execution plan.

---

## Responsibilities

The planner:

* Breaks the goal into milestones
* Creates executable tasks
* Orders work logically
* Estimates effort
* Detects dependencies
* Minimizes unnecessary work

---

## Planning Principles

Always:

* Reuse existing work
* Keep iterations small
* Reduce risk
* Deliver value early

---

## Output

The planner creates:

* Milestones
* Tasks
* Dependencies
* Suggested Loops
* Required Agents
* Estimated Completion
