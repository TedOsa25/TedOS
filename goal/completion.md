# Goal Completion

## Purpose

Determine whether a goal has truly been completed.

---

## Completion Checklist

A goal is complete only if:

* Business objective achieved
* Success criteria met
* Reviews passed
* Documentation updated
* Runtime updated
* Memory updated
* No blocking issues remain

---

## Final Actions

When complete:

* Archive execution history
* Update knowledge
* Update memory
* Close workflows
* Close loops
* Notify the orchestrator

---

## Final Question

After completion:
"What is the next highest-impact goal?"
