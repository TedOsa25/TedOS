# Goal Queue

## Purpose

Manage all active and future goals.

---

## States

* Backlog
* Ready
* Active
* Waiting
* Blocked
* Review
* Completed
* Archived

---

## Prioritization

Goals are ordered by:

* Business Impact
* Customer Impact
* Revenue
* Risk
* Dependencies
* Urgency

---

## Rule

Only one highest-priority goal should be actively executed at a time unless parallel execution is explicitly planned through Worktrees.
