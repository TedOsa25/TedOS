# Gmail Connector

## Purpose

The Gmail Connector enables TedOS to manage email communication.

---

## Responsibilities

* Read Emails
* Send Emails
* Create Drafts
* Reply to Emails
* Search Mailbox
* Apply Labels
* Archive Emails
* Monitor Inbox

---

## Inputs

* Email
* Thread
* Attachment
* Label
* Search Query

---

## Outputs

* Email Status
* Draft Status
* Thread Status
* Label Status

---

## Security Rules

* Never send emails without approval unless explicitly automated.
* Never expose confidential email content.
* Respect mailbox permissions.

---

## Typical Workflows

* Customer Communication
* Sales Outreach
* Follow-ups
* Support
* Notifications
