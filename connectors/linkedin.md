# LinkedIn Connector

## Purpose

The LinkedIn Connector enables TedOS to manage LinkedIn content and company presence.

---

## Responsibilities

* Create Posts
* Schedule Posts
* Read Company Pages
* Monitor Engagement
* Analyze Performance
* Support Social Selling

---

## Inputs

* Company Page
* Post
* Campaign
* Analytics

---

## Outputs

* Post Status
* Engagement Metrics
* Performance Reports

---

## Security Rules

* Never publish content without approval.
* Respect brand guidelines.
* Protect account credentials.

---

## Typical Workflows

* Company Updates
* Product Launches
* Thought Leadership
* Employer Branding
* Sales Content
