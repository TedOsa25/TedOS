# Slack Connector

## Purpose

The Slack Connector enables TedOS to communicate with teams through Slack.

---

## Responsibilities

* Read Channels
* Send Messages
* Reply to Threads
* Create Channels
* Notify Teams
* Share Reports
* Monitor Notifications

---

## Inputs

* Channel
* Message
* Thread
* User

---

## Outputs

* Message Status
* Notification Status
* Channel Status

---

## Security Rules

* Never send messages to public channels without approval.
* Respect workspace permissions.
* Protect confidential information.

---

## Typical Workflows

* Team Notifications
* Deployment Updates
* Sales Alerts
* Customer Support
* Internal Communication
