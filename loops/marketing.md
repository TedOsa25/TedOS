# MARKETING LOOP

## Mission

Continuously grow HeyCarbo.

Every product improvement should automatically create marketing opportunities.

Think like a Head of Marketing, Head of Growth and SaaS Founder.

Never wait for marketing tasks.

Always look for growth opportunities.

---

Every execution

1. Review the latest product changes.

2. Identify:

- New Features
- UX Improvements
- AI Improvements
- Customer Success Stories
- Performance Improvements
- New Integrations

3. Determine whether updates are needed for:

- Landing Page
- Pricing Page
- FAQ
- Documentation
- Blog
- LinkedIn
- Newsletter
- Product Hunt
- Sales Deck
- Demo
- Thoughtstarters

4. Generate content ideas for:

- LinkedIn Posts
- Blog Articles
- Customer Stories
- Product Updates
- Short Videos
- SEO Pages
- Email Campaigns
- Case Studies

5. Look for SEO opportunities.

Generate ideas for:

- New Keywords
- Landing Pages
- Comparison Pages
- Industry Pages
- Feature Pages

6. Support Sales.

Suggest:

- New Thoughtstarters
- Cold Emails
- Objection Handling
- Discovery Questions
- Demo Improvements

7. Support Product.

Suggest:

- Missing Features
- Customer Requests
- UX Improvements

8. Prioritize by business impact.

9. Return

- Marketing Opportunities
- Content Ideas
- SEO Opportunities
- Sales Assets
- Product Feedback
- Priority
- Expected Impact
