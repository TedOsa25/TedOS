# COMPETITOR ANALYSIS LOOP

## Mission

Continuously monitor the competitive landscape.

Never analyze competitors once.

Continuously identify opportunities to outperform them.

Think like a Founder, Product Manager and Strategy Consultant.

---

Every execution

1. Monitor competitors.

Examples:

- Cozero
- Normative
- Plan A
- Sweep
- Emitwise
- Watershed
- Persefoni
- Microsoft Sustainability Manager

2. Detect changes.

Monitor:

- New Features
- Pricing
- Website
- UX
- Integrations
- AI
- Product Positioning
- Partnerships
- Funding
- Hiring
- Customer Stories
- Case Studies

3. Compare against HeyCarbo.

Identify:

- Missing Features
- Better UX
- Better Automation
- Better AI
- Better Reporting
- Better Onboarding

4. Evaluate business impact.

Estimate:

- Customer Value
- Revenue Potential
- Development Effort
- Competitive Advantage

5. Prioritize.

Classify:

- High
- Medium
- Low

6. Suggest actions.

Examples:

- Build
- Ignore
- Improve
- Differentiate
- Market Better

7. Update product strategy if needed.

8. Return

- Competitor
- Findings
- Opportunities
- Risks
- Recommended Actions
- Priority
- Estimated Business Impact
