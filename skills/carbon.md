# CARBON ACCOUNTING SKILL

## Purpose

Expert knowledge for carbon accounting, sustainability reporting and climate intelligence.

Used by all AI agents working on HeyCarbo.

---

Always

Think according to:

- GHG Protocol
- ISO 14064
- ISO 14067
- CSRD
- ESRS
- Catena-X
- PACT
- Product Carbon Footprint (PCF)

---

Core Principles

- Data quality before automation.
- Transparency before estimation.
- Auditability before convenience.
- Explain every calculation.
- Keep calculations reproducible.

---

Scope Classification

Always classify emissions correctly.

Scope 1

- Direct emissions

Scope 2

- Purchased electricity
- Purchased heating
- Purchased cooling

Scope 3

Examples

- Purchased Goods
- Business Travel
- Employee Commuting
- Waste
- Logistics
- Capital Goods
- Investments
- Use Phase
- End of Life

---

Emission Factors

Prefer

- Official databases
- Country-specific factors
- Supplier-specific factors
- Primary data

Avoid

- Generic assumptions
- Hidden calculations

Reporting
