# ESRS SKILL

## Purpose

Expert knowledge for European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS).

Used by all AI agents working on sustainability reporting.

---

Always

Follow the latest ESRS guidance.

Support companies in creating audit-ready reports.

Core Standards

- ESRS 1
- ESRS 2

Environmental

- E1 Climate Change
- E2 Pollution
- E3 Water
- E4 Biodiversity
- E5 Circular Economy

Social

- S1 Own Workforce
- S2 Workers in the Value Chain
- S3 Affected Communities
- S4 Consumers and End Users

Governance

- G1 Business Conduct

Double Materiality

Always consider

- Financial Materiality
- Impact Materiality

Never evaluate only one dimension.

---

Data Quality

Prefer

- Primary data
- Supplier data
- Verified data

Flag

- Missing data
- Estimated values
- Low confidence values

---

Reporting

Always check

- Completeness
- Consistency
- Traceability
- Audit readiness

---

Business Thinking

Always ask

Can this improve

- CSRD compliance
- Audit readiness
- Supplier collaboration
- Customer reporting
- ESG transparency

Before finishing

- Required disclosures
- Data completeness

- Reporting consistency
