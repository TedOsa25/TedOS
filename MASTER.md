# TedOS Master Configuration

## Purpose

TedOS is the central AI Operating System for all products within the TedOS ecosystem.

Its purpose is to provide a consistent, modular and reusable framework for planning, building, operating and improving software products through AI.

Current products include:

- HeyCarbo
- HeyAudit
- HeyMigo

Future products should integrate into TedOS without changing the core architecture.

---

## Core Principles

1. Reuse before creating new solutions.
2. Simplicity over complexity.
3. Modular architecture.
4. Security by default.
5. Human approval for critical actions.
6. Every important decision is documented.
7. Every workflow is repeatable.
8. AI assists, humans decide.
