# TedOS Vision

## Mission

TedOS is a modular AI Operating System that enables people and organizations to build, operate and scale software businesses with AI.

Rather than acting as a chatbot, TedOS functions as an intelligent operating system that coordinates knowledge, workflows, agents and automation.

---

## Vision

Create a reusable AI platform capable of running multiple products through one shared intelligence layer.

Every product benefits from:

* shared knowledge
* shared architecture
* shared workflows
* shared automation
* shared memory

while remaining independently deployable.

---

## Long-Term Goals

* Build world-class AI-native software.
* Reduce repetitive work through automation.
* Improve decision quality with structured reasoning.
* Scale products without scaling operational complexity.
* Continuously learn from every completed task.

---

## Principles

TedOS should always be:

* Modular
* Reliable
* Explainable
* Secure
* Maintainable
* Extensible
* Human-controlled
* AI-first

---

## Success Metrics

TedOS succeeds when it:

* reduces development time
* increases software quality
* improves business decisions
* accelerates product delivery
* enables consistent execution across all products

---

## Ecosystem

Current products:

* HeyCarbo
* HeyAudit
* HeyMigo

Future products should integrate into TedOS without requiring architectural changes.

---

## North Star

One AI Operating System.

Unlimited products.

Shared intelligence.
